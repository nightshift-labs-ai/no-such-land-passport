# Halqará Suyu — Republic of Halqara (code HLQ)

A high mountain republic on a cold upland plateau (altiplano, 3,800–5,100 m) ringed by snow passes.
Wool, salt and potatoes; herders, weavers, wind. Roads close under snow from November to April.

- **Own language (Halqareñ):** Latin script with ñ, ë, á; words end in -ë / -a / -q.
  Pasaq = passport, Suyu = republic, Ayllë = surname, Sutikë = given names, Llaqtë = nationality,
  Paqarë = birth, Warhë = sex, Quyë = issue, Tukuyë = expiry, Kamachë = authority, Qillqë = signature,
  Willqë = visas, Lluqsiy = departure, Yaykuy = arrival, Apachë Ñan = high pass road.
- **Capital and issuing city:** Qorimarka (3,910 m) — Pasaq Wasi (Passport House).
- **Holder:** QHAPAÑA, Illarë Sisa — born 14 Mar 1988 in Sallqapata (a herding village, 4,120 m).
  MRZ transliteration: Ñ→N, Ë→E (QHAPANA<<ILLARE<SISA).
- **Neighbours:**
  - Mayupampa (MYP) — warm river lowlands to the east; river posts, ferries. Words: Admeso = arrival, Eksido = departure.
  - Chaskani (CHK) — salt-flat federation to the south. Words: Kirï = arrival, Ulë = departure.
- **Telling detail:** an *Apachë Ñan / High Pass Permit* endorsement: valid only in the dry season
  (1 May – 31 Oct), void when a pass is declared closed by snow, and the fee is
  **paid in kind: 2 skeins (500 g) of alpaca wool**, stamped PAKTASQA / PAID IN KIND.
